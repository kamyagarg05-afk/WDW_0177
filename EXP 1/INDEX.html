Introduction

The B.Tech Computer Science program is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in computer science principles, programming languages, algorithms, and software development. The curriculum emphasizes both theoretical knowledge and practical skills to prepare students for careers in the rapidly evolving field of computer science.

  2. Physics I

  5. Basic Electrical Engineering

DATABASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM, a software application that interacts with user data and databases.
